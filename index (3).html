Everything You Need to Know

Are classes suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Beginners can start with Noorani Qaida and gradually progress to Quran reading.

Can children and adults both enroll?

Yes. The programs are designed to accommodate different ages and experience levels.

Are the classes one-to-one?

One-to-one learning is featured in our approach. Please confirm class arrangements when you enquire.

How do I schedule a trial class?

Fill out the inquiry form below and choose WhatsApp or Email. Your message opens in the selected app for you to send.

What do I need for online lessons?

A stable internet connection and a phone, tablet, or computer are generally enough. Your teacher can advise on lesson materials.
